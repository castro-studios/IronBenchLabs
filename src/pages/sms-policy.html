SMS Opt-In Policy

How to Opt In

When you create an account, join a team, or enter your phone number for IronBench Labs notifications, you will see an unchecked SMS consent checkbox:

☐ I agree to receive SMS text messages from IronBench Labs related to team invites, RSVP links, game reminders, lineup updates, and account notifications. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not required to use IronBench Labs.

You must intentionally check this box to opt in to SMS messages. The checkbox is unchecked by default. Your consent is completely separate from accepting our Terms of Service or Privacy Policy.

What Messages You'll Receive

By opting in to SMS, you agree to receive text messages from IronBench Labs for:

  • Account notifications (verification codes, account alerts)
  • Team invitations and RSVP links
  • Game reminders and lineup updates
  • Customer support messages
  • Team management alerts

Message Frequency & Costs

Message frequency varies depending on your activity. Standard message and data rates apply from your wireless carrier. You are responsible for any charges imposed by your mobile service provider.

How to Opt Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time:

  • Reply STOP to any text message from IronBench Labs to unsubscribe
  • Update your account settings to disable SMS notifications

Privacy & Data

SMS consent and phone numbers are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Your phone number is used solely to deliver the messages you've opted into. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
